Privacy Policy

What We Collect

Language Soup collects minimal data to provide our service:

  • Profile information: Name, profile photo (optional), languages you're learning

  • Usage data: App activity to improve the experience

Camera Permission

We request camera access only to let you take profile photos. This is optional - you can skip it or upload from your photo library instead.

How We Use Your Data

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers. We never sell your information to third parties.
